Build Your Wireless Network

Your bees already work without wires — your sensors should too. Hive data, straight to the cloud. Choose the right connectivity for your scenario — LoRaWAN® for long-range, low-power applications; Cellular for areas without gateway coverage.

LoRaWAN® (Long Range Wide Area Network) is a wireless protocol purpose-built for IoT. It delivers exceptional range — up to 15 km in rural areas — while keeping power consumption so low that a single battery can last years. Designed for devices that send small amounts of data infrequently, LoRaWAN® is the go-to choice for remote, hard-to-reach, and large-scale sensor deployments.

LoRaWAN® Network Topology

Cellular IoT protocols — NB-IoT & LTE-M (CAT-M1) & CAT-1 — bring your sensor data to the cloud over existing mobile networks. Unlike LoRaWAN®, there are no gateways to deploy or maintain. A cellular sensor works anywhere there's a cell tower, making it the plug-and-play choice for beekeepers who want zero infrastructure and instant connectivity.
